# THK-0018 - Non-Newtonian Foam Behaviour

## Definition

Foam flow behaviour in which viscosity is not constant with shear rate.

## Core Idea

Shear-thinning and yield-like behaviour motivate specialised constitutive models.

## Why It Matters

Provides the physical rationale for conventional rheological models.

## Structure / Characteristics

Shear condition → bubble rearrangement/deformation → changing apparent viscosity.
