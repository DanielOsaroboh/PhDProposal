# THK-0010 - Temperature Effect on Foam Stability

## Phenomenon

The causal influence of temperature on drainage, viscosity and foam stability.

## Starting Condition

Elevated temperature can accelerate liquid drainage and reduce structural persistence.

## Causal Chain

```text
Temperature ↑ → drainage ↑ → film/structure persistence ↓ → stability and often viscosity ↓.
```

## Why the Mechanism Matters

Explains why moderate-temperature results cannot simply be assumed to hold at HPHT conditions.

## Governing Variables

The strength and direction of the mechanism can depend on pressure, temperature, shear, foam quality, salinity, concentration, and formulation chemistry as relevant.

## Observable Outcome

The mechanism becomes visible through changes in foam structure, apparent viscosity, stability, drainage, or downstream engineering behaviour.

## Boundary Conditions

The causal pathway should not be assumed to have identical strength across formulations or outside tested operating ranges.

## Evidence

Hutchins and Miller (2005); Fu and Liu (2021).
