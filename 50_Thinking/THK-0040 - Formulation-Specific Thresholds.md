# THK-0040 - Formulation-Specific Thresholds

## What Is Limited

The restriction that optimum or transition values depend on the particular nanoparticle–surfactant formulation.

## Nature of the Limitation

A threshold found in one chemistry cannot automatically be transferred to another.

## Limitation Pathway

```text
Different chemistry → different interaction strength/regime → different threshold.
```

## Why It Matters

Requires domain-specific validation and cautious generalisation.

## Conditions Where It Matters Most

The limitation becomes especially important under sparse, unfamiliar, extrapolated, or formulation-shifted conditions.

## Consequence

Uncritical use can create overconfidence in model transfer, generalisation, or engineering interpretation.

## Mitigation Direction

Use domain-aware validation, uncertainty assessment, explicit reporting of operating ranges, and further experimental evaluation where needed.
