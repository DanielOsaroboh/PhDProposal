# THK-0121 - Integrated Research Contribution Architecture

## Definition

The Integrated Research Contribution Architecture represents how the
individual expected outputs of the doctoral research combine into a
coherent petroleum-engineering contribution.

## Core Idea

The research contribution does not arise from one isolated model,
dataset, or uncertainty method.

It emerges from the integration of several connected outputs.

## Scientific Boundary

The collective contribution is intended to support further laboratory  
and engineering evaluation.

It should not be represented as providing automatically field-ready  
fracturing-fluid design decisions.

## Contribution Principle

The strongest claim is therefore not:

> The research will determine the optimal field fracturing-fluid design.

Rather:

> The research is expected to provide an uncertainty-aware predictive  
> framework for estimating foam behaviour, characterising prediction  
> reliability, and identifying candidate operating conditions for  
> subsequent engineering evaluation.
