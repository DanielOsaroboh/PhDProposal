# THK-0005 - Foam Apparent Viscosity

## Definition

The effective viscosity exhibited by a non-Newtonian foam at specified flow and shear conditions.

## What It Represents

It expresses flow resistance and is central to suspension and transport performance.

## Why It Matters

One of the primary predicted properties in the proposed research.

## Governing Variables

Pressure/temperature/shear/quality/formulation → foam structure → apparent viscosity → transport response.

### Multivariable Control under HPHT Conditions

Apparent viscosity should not be treated as the response to a single
operating variable.

Under HPHT conditions it may be governed by coupled interactions among:

- pressure;
- temperature;
- [[THK-0015 - Foam Quality]];
- surfactant chemistry;
- nanoparticle concentration;
- salinity;
- shear rate;
- gas-phase characteristics.

The predictive structure is therefore:

Pressure
    +
Temperature
    +
Foam quality
    +
Surfactant chemistry
    +
Nanoparticle concentration
    +
Salinity
    +
Shear rate
    +
Gas-phase characteristics
        ↓
Coupled nonlinear interactions
        ↓
Apparent viscosity

This multivariable dependence means that viscosity measured or predicted
under one condition should not automatically be assumed transferable to
another HPHT condition.

**Evidence:** Al-Darweesh et al. (2024); Ghorbani (2025).


## Measurement / Interpretation

The property should be interpreted at explicitly stated pressure, temperature, shear, foam quality, salinity, and formulation conditions where applicable.

## Engineering Significance

Changes in this property can alter downstream suspension, transport, or operating-window assessment.

## Boundaries

A measured or predicted value is meaningful only within its experimental or model domain and associated uncertainty.
