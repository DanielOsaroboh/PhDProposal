# THK-0009 - Pressure Effect on Foam Rheology

## Phenomenon

The causal influence of pressure on foam rheology through gas-phase and bubble-structure changes.

## Starting Condition

Pressure changes gas behaviour and bubble size/structure, which changes apparent viscosity.

## Causal Chain

```text
Pressure change → gas compression/phase behaviour → bubble structure change → viscosity response.
```

## Why the Mechanism Matters

Makes pressure a governing predictor of HPHT foam behaviour.

## Governing Variables

The strength and direction of the mechanism can depend on pressure, temperature, shear, foam quality, salinity, concentration, and formulation chemistry as relevant.

## Observable Outcome

The mechanism becomes visible through changes in foam structure, apparent viscosity, stability, drainage, or downstream engineering behaviour.

## Boundary Conditions

The causal pathway should not be assumed to have identical strength across formulations or outside tested operating ranges.
