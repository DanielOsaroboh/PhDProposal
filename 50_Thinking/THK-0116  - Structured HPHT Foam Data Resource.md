# THK-0116 - Structured HPHT Foam Data Resource

## Contribution Statement

The research is expected to produce a structured HPHT foam data
resource derived from the systematic extraction, digitisation,
harmonisation, and standardisation of available experimental evidence.

## Problem Addressed

Existing HPHT foam rheology and stability evidence is frequently:

- heterogeneous;
- distributed across different studies;
- formulation-specific;
- reported using different units and conventions;
- not immediately suitable for machine-learning analysis.

The contribution transforms this fragmented evidence into a more
structured analytical resource.

## Contribution Process

```text
Published Experimental Evidence
        ↓
Systematic Extraction
        ↓
Digitisation
        ↓
Harmonisation
        ↓
Standardisation
        ↓
Quality-Assured Structure
        ↓
Structured HPHT Foam Data Resource

```

## Intended Content

The resource may contain variables describing:

### Operating Conditions

- pressure;
- temperature;
- shear conditions.

### Foam and Formulation Conditions

- foam quality;
- salinity;
- surfactant concentration;
- nanoparticle concentration;
- other available formulation descriptors.

### Response Variables

- apparent viscosity;
- foam-stability indicators.

## Scientific Value

The contribution is not simply the accumulation of numerical  
observations.

Its value lies in creating a structured representation of heterogeneous  
experimental evidence suitable for:

- exploratory analysis;
- comparative analysis;
- predictive modelling;
- uncertainty analysis;
- reproducible research.

## Relationship to Existing Methods

The contribution is produced through:

- [[THK-0072 - HPHT Foam Dataset Compilation and Structuring]]
- [[THK-0085 - Heterogeneous Foam Dataset Harmonisation]]
- [[THK-0111 - Literature Data Digitisation]]

These are **methods**.

The structured dataset resulting from them is the **contribution**.

## Important Boundary

The breadth of the resource will depend on the availability and  
adequacy of published experimental evidence.

Therefore, the contribution should not imply comprehensive coverage of  
all possible HPHT foam formulations or operating conditions.
