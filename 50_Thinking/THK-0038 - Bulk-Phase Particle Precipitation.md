# THK-0038 - Bulk-Phase Particle Precipitation

## Failure Mechanism Definition

Retention or precipitation of aggregated particles in the liquid bulk rather than at the stabilising interface.

## Desired State

This reduces effective interfacial particle availability.

## Failure Chain

```text
Excess aggregation → bulk-phase particle presence → interface coverage ↓ → stability ↓.
```

## Trigger

The failure is initiated when operating or formulation conditions move into an unfavourable regime.

## Observable Consequence

The system exhibits reduced stability, poorer suspension, precipitation, or another loss of desired performance.

## Engineering Significance

Explains performance decline beyond favourable formulation ranges.

## Prevention / Mitigation Logic

Identify the conditions associated with the failure, avoid unsupported extrapolation, and use candidate operating windows for targeted validation.

## Boundary Conditions

The exact trigger and severity are formulation- and condition-dependent.
