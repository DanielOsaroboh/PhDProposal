# THK-0011 - Shear Effect on Foam Behaviour

## Phenomenon

The influence of imposed shear on foam microstructure and rheological response.

## Starting Condition

Because foam is non-Newtonian, its apparent viscosity and structure depend on shear conditions.

## Causal Chain

```text
Shear-rate change → bubble deformation/rearrangement → rheological response change.
```

## Why the Mechanism Matters

Requires shear conditions to be represented in modelling and interpretation.

## Governing Variables

The strength and direction of the mechanism can depend on pressure, temperature, shear, foam quality, salinity, concentration, and formulation chemistry as relevant.

## Observable Outcome

The mechanism becomes visible through changes in foam structure, apparent viscosity, stability, drainage, or downstream engineering behaviour.

## Boundary Conditions

The causal pathway should not be assumed to have identical strength across formulations or outside tested operating ranges.

## Evidence

Sinha et al. (2019); Ahmed et al. (2017).
