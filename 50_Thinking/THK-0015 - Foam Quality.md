# THK-0015 - Foam Quality

## Definition

The volumetric gas fraction of a foam.

## Core Idea

Foam quality strongly influences bubble packing, structure and rheological regime.

## Why It Matters

A central structural predictor in energised-foam modelling.

## Structure / Characteristics

Gas fraction change → structural change → viscosity/stability response.
