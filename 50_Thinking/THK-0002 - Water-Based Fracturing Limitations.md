# THK-0002 - Water-Based Fracturing Limitations

## Problem Definition

Conventional water-based fracturing can create water-use and formation-compatibility problems.

## Context

High freshwater demand and water invasion can be undesirable in water-sensitive formations.

## Problem Logic

```text
Water loading/invasion → clay or formation interaction → formation-damage risk → possible productivity loss.
```

## Why It Matters

Motivates alternative energised-fluid systems.

## Causes / Drivers

The problem arises from interacting experimental, physical, formulation, data, or modelling constraints represented in the linked notes.

## Consequences

## Why It Persists

## Research Implication
