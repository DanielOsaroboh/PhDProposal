# THK-0049 - Operating-Window Identification

## Decision Objective

A structured process for identifying combinations of conditions and formulations that satisfy performance criteria with acceptable predictive reliability.

## Core Decision Logic

It combines predictions, uncertainty, engineering criteria and validation needs.

## Process

```text
Define domain/criteria → predict properties → quantify uncertainty → screen conditions → identify candidates → validate.
```

## Required Inputs

Operating/formulation variables, predicted apparent viscosity and stability, uncertainty information, and engineering performance criteria.

## Decision Criteria

Candidate conditions should satisfy defined property requirements while also having acceptable predictive support.

## Uncertainty Treatment

Conditions with high uncertainty should be flagged for caution or further testing rather than treated as equally reliable.

## Output

A set or region of candidate conditions for laboratory and engineering evaluation.

## Validation

Candidate windows require subsequent experimental or engineering validation; they are not final proof of field performance.

## Evidence

Critical synthesis of supplied literature.

## Evidence Discipline

The cited sources are retained from the supplied thesis background. Before final academic use, bibliographic details and the exact scope of each supporting claim should be checked against the original publications.

## Role in the Research

Operating-window identification represents a major engineering output
of the predictive framework.

The research seeks to move from:

Prediction
        ↓
Property value

toward:

Prediction
+
Uncertainty
+
Reservoir constraints
+
Treatment constraints
        ↓
Candidate operating window

The operating window therefore represents a decision layer constructed
from model outputs rather than a direct machine-learning output.

Its purpose is to identify combinations of formulation and operating
conditions that warrant further laboratory and engineering evaluation.
