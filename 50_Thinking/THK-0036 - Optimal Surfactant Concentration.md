# THK-0036 - Optimal Surfactant Concentration

## Threshold Definition

A concentration region where beneficial nanoparticle–surfactant interaction is maximised before excessive aggregation becomes detrimental.

## Core Idea

The response can improve, peak and then decline rather than increasing monotonically.

## Behaviour Across the Threshold

```text
Below optimum: stabilisation rises → optimum: beneficial balance → above: excessive flocculation → stability falls.
```

## Below the Threshold

The system remains in the lower-regime behaviour described by the available evidence.

## Near the Threshold

Competing structural or physicochemical effects become important and the response may become strongly nonlinear.

## Above the Threshold

A different regime or adverse mechanism may dominate; the exact behaviour is formulation- and condition-dependent.

## Engineering Significance

Directly supports operating-window identification.

## Boundary Conditions

The threshold should be treated as conditional rather than a universal constant.
