# THK-0016 - Foam Quality–Viscosity Relationship

## Relationship Definition

The relationship between volumetric gas fraction and apparent viscosity.

## Variables Involved

Viscosity can increase with foam quality up to a critical regime, after which behaviour may change.

## Relationship Pattern

```text
Foam quality ↑ → structural crowding ↑ → viscosity ↑ → critical region → altered response.
```

## Mechanistic Interpretation

The observed relationship should be interpreted through the mechanisms represented in the linked THK notes rather than as correlation alone.

## Engineering Significance

Demonstrates nonlinear behaviour requiring flexible representation.

## Moderators and Boundary Conditions

Pressure, temperature, shear, foam quality, salinity, formulation, particle characteristics, and experimental method may modify the relationship.

## Caution

Direction and magnitude should not be assumed universal unless supported across the relevant operating domain.
