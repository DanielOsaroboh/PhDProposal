# THK-0003 - Water-Sensitive Reservoirs

## Definition

Reservoirs in which introduced water may adversely affect formation properties or productivity.

## Core Idea

Water interaction with susceptible minerals can make conventional water-heavy stimulation undesirable.

## Why It Matters

Defines a key application environment for energised foams.

## Structure / Characteristics

Water exposure → mineral/fluid interaction → possible swelling/damage → impaired flow.

## Governing Variables / Conditions
