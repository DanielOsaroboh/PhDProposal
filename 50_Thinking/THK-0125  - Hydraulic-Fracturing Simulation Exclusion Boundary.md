# THK-0125 - Hydraulic-Fracturing Simulation Exclusion Boundary

## Limitation Statement

The research does not attempt to develop a fully coupled
three-dimensional hydraulic-fracturing simulator.

## Why This Is a Boundary

The predictive framework focuses on a narrower engineering problem:

> prediction and reliability assessment of HPHT energised-foam
> rheology and stability.

It does not attempt to reproduce the complete physical evolution of a
hydraulic-fracturing treatment.

## Scope Separation

```text
THIS RESEARCH

HPHT Foam Data
        ↓
Rheology + Stability Prediction
        ↓
Uncertainty Quantification
        ↓
Candidate Operating Windows
        ↓
Engineering Assessment

--------------------------------

OUTSIDE DIRECT SCOPE

Fully Coupled 3D Hydraulic-Fracturing Simulation
````

## What the Framework Can Support

Outputs may inform:

- fracturing-fluid design assessment;
- proppant suspension assessment;
- proppant transport assessment;
- candidate operating-window identification;
- existing fracture-design workflows.

## What the Framework Does Not Replace

It does not replace:

- full fracture simulation;
- experimental validation;
- complete fracture-design workflows;
- engineering judgement.

## Methodological Importance

Explicitly excluding full 3D fracture simulation prevents the research  
from becoming excessively broad.

The PhD remains centred on:

Foam Behaviour  
+  
Prediction  
+  
Uncertainty  
+  
Engineering Interpretation

rather than attempting simultaneously to solve the complete  
hydraulic-fracturing simulation problem.

## Contribution Boundary

The framework's contribution should therefore be expressed as:

> support for further laboratory and engineering evaluation

rather than:

> complete prediction of field hydraulic-fracturing performance.

## Relationship to Field Validation

This boundary reinforces:

[[THK-0108 - Laboratory-to-Field Validation Boundary]]

because even a reliable foam-property prediction does not independently  
validate complete field treatment performance.

## Relationship to Evidence

The exclusion also recognises the limitations of available datasets.

Existing HPHT foam rheology and stability evidence does not necessarily  
contain all information required to construct or validate a fully  
coupled 3D fracture simulator.
