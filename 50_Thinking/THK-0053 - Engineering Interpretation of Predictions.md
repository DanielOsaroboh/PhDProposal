# THK-0053 - Engineering Interpretation of Predictions

## Principle Statement

The principle that model outputs should be interpreted through engineering significance, uncertainty, boundaries and downstream consequences.

## Meaning

Prediction quality is not only numerical accuracy; decision relevance depends on reliability and operational meaning.

## Interpretive Logic

```text
Prediction + uncertainty + domain knowledge + criteria → engineering interpretation.
```

## Rationale

Engineering decisions require more than numerical prediction; they require understanding of reliability, operating domain, and downstream consequences.

## Application

Prevents ML metrics from becoming detached from fracturing-fluid design.

## Boundaries

Engineering interpretation should not be used to overstate what the model or dataset supports.

## Implication

Model evaluation should include decision relevance and uncertainty, not predictive accuracy alone.

## Research Application

Engineering interpretation forms the final translation layer of the
research.

Model outputs are evaluated in relation to:

- fracturing-fluid design;
- operating-condition selection;
- proppant transport assessment.

The analytical progression is:

Model Output
        ↓
Uncertainty / Reliability
        ↓
Engineering Criteria
        ↓
Interpretation
        ↓
Candidate Decision Support

This preserves an important boundary:

The predictive framework supports engineering evaluation; it does not
replace complete hydraulic-fracturing design or field validation.
