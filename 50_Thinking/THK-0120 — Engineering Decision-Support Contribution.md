# THK-0120 - Engineering Decision-Support Contribution

## Contribution Statement

The research is expected to provide engineering decision-support
information by connecting predicted foam rheology, stability,
predictive uncertainty, and candidate operating windows to
fracturing-fluid design and downstream proppant-transport assessment.

## Core Contribution

The contribution translates computational outputs into petroleum
engineering information.

```text
Foam Predictions
        +
Prediction Uncertainty
        +
Candidate Operating Windows
        ↓
Engineering Interpretation
        ↓
Fracturing-Fluid Design Assessment
        +
Proppant Suspension / Transport Assessment
````

## Why Translation Matters

A machine-learning prediction has limited engineering value if it  
remains disconnected from the physical decisions for which the  
prediction is intended.

The contribution therefore connects:

> predictive performance

to:

> engineering relevance.

## Engineering Areas

The research considers implications for:

- fracturing-fluid design;
- formulation assessment;
- operating-condition assessment;
- proppant suspension;
- proppant transport.

## Role of Operating Windows

[[THK-0103 - Uncertainty-Bounded Operating Window]] provides an  
intermediate decision object between model output and engineering  
assessment.

Thus:

Prediction  
↓  
Reliability  
↓  
Candidate Operating Window  
↓  
Engineering Assessment

## Role of Engineering Judgement

The framework does not replace engineering judgement.

Instead:

Model Output  
+  
Uncertainty Information  
+  
Engineering Constraints  
+  
Engineering Judgement  
↓  
Decision Support

## Scope Boundary

The contribution does **not** claim to provide automatically field-ready  
hydraulic-fracturing designs.

The outputs are intended to support:

- further laboratory evaluation;
- engineering evaluation;
- subsequent validation.

## Why This Boundary Matters

The distinction prevents:

Candidate Operating Window  
≠  
Certified Field Operating Envelope

and:

Predictive Framework  
≠  
Autonomous Treatment Design System
