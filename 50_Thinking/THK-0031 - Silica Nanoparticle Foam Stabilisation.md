# THK-0031 - Silica Nanoparticle Foam Stabilisation

## Phenomenon

Stabilisation of foam through silica-particle reinforcement of the gas–liquid interface.

## Starting Condition

Interfacial particles can enhance film elasticity and retard liquid drainage.

## Causal Chain

```text
Silica accumulation at interface → stronger interfacial barrier → drainage ↓ → stability ↑.
```

## Why the Mechanism Matters

Physical basis for nanoparticle-enhanced foam formulations.

## Governing Variables

The strength and direction of the mechanism can depend on pressure, temperature, shear, foam quality, salinity, concentration, and formulation chemistry as relevant.

## Observable Outcome

The mechanism becomes visible through changes in foam structure, apparent viscosity, stability, drainage, or downstream engineering behaviour.

## Boundary Conditions

The causal pathway should not be assumed to have identical strength across formulations or outside tested operating ranges.

## Evidence

Gonzalez Perdomo and Wan Madihi (2022).
