# THK-0017 - Critical Foam Quality

## Threshold Definition

A transition range in foam quality at which rheological behaviour changes as the structure becomes increasingly gas dominated.

## Core Idea

It is a regime boundary rather than necessarily a universal fixed value.

## Behaviour Across the Threshold

```text
Below: rising structural effects → near threshold: transition → above: changed gas-dominated rheology.
```

## Below the Threshold

The system remains in the lower-regime behaviour described by the available evidence.

## Near the Threshold

Competing structural or physicochemical effects become important and the response may become strongly nonlinear.

## Above the Threshold

A different regime or adverse mechanism may dominate; the exact behaviour is formulation- and condition-dependent.

## Engineering Significance

Relevant to defining safe or useful operating regions.

## Boundary Conditions

The threshold should be treated as conditional rather than a universal constant.
