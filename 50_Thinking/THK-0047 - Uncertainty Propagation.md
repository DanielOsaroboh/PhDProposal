# THK-0047 - Uncertainty Propagation

## Phenomenon

Transfer of uncertainty in upstream predicted properties into downstream engineering assessments.

## Starting Condition

Uncertain viscosity/stability predictions produce uncertain proppant and performance interpretations.

## Causal Chain

```text
Property uncertainty → transport-assessment uncertainty → decision uncertainty.
```

## Why the Mechanism Matters

Connects statistical uncertainty to engineering consequences.

## Governing Variables

The strength and direction of the mechanism can depend on pressure, temperature, shear, foam quality, salinity, concentration, and formulation chemistry as relevant.

## Observable Outcome

The mechanism becomes visible through changes in foam structure, apparent viscosity, stability, drainage, or downstream engineering behaviour.

## Boundary Conditions

The causal pathway should not be assumed to have identical strength across formulations or outside tested operating ranges.

## Evidence

Tran et al. (2025).

### Extended Engineering Propagation Chain

Predictive uncertainty can propagate beyond the immediate foam-property
prediction.

A broader engineering pathway is:

Uncertainty in HPHT foam behaviour
        ↓
Uncertainty in apparent viscosity / foam stability
        ↓
Uncertainty in fracturing-fluid formulation decisions
        ↓
Uncertainty in operating-condition selection
        ↓
Uncertainty in proppant suspension and transport assessment
        ↓
Uncertainty in fracture-performance interpretation
        ↓
Uncertainty in overall treatment-performance assessment

This establishes an important distinction between **prediction
uncertainty** and **engineering consequence uncertainty**.

The former concerns confidence in the predicted property.

The latter concerns how uncertainty in that property affects subsequent
engineering reasoning and decisions.

**Evidence:** Tran et al. (2025).
