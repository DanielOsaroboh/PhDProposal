# THK-0014 - Limited HPHT Experimental Coverage

## Gap Statement

Existing experiments incompletely cover the extreme and coupled pressure-temperature domain relevant to HPHT reservoirs.

## What Is Known

Moderate-temperature studies are valuable but leave sparse evidence at more extreme coupled conditions.

## What Is Missing

Restricted experimental range → sparse HPHT domain → unsupported regions → uncertain extrapolation.

## Why the Gap Matters

Creates a direct empirical gap affecting predictive reliability.

## Why Existing Evidence Is Insufficient

Existing studies provide valuable pieces of evidence but do not fully cover the required operating domain or integrate all required predictive and engineering dimensions.

## Consequence of the Gap

Engineering decisions may be based on predictions whose reliability under target HPHT conditions is unclear.

## Research Need

Develop and evaluate an approach that addresses the missing empirical, predictive, uncertainty, and engineering-integration dimensions without claiming validity beyond available evidence.

## Evidence

Fu and Liu (2021); Lv et al. (2015); Gonzalez Perdomo and Wan Madihi (2022).
