# THK-0124 - Foam Half-Life as Stability Operationalisation

## Definition

Foam half-life is the primary measure through which foam stability is
operationalised within the predictive scope of the research.

## Conceptual Distinction

Foam stability is the broader engineering concept:

[[THK-0006 - Foam Stability]]

Foam half-life provides a measurable indicator through which that
concept can be represented computationally.

Therefore:

```text
Foam Stability
Broad Physical Concept
        ↓
Operationalised Through
        ↓
Foam Half-Life
Measurable Predictive Target
````

## Role in the Research

The machine-learning framework requires defined response variables.

For stability:

```
HPHT + Formulation Inputs
        ↓
Predictive Model
        ↓
Foam Half-Life
        ↓
Indicator of Foam Stability
```

## Why Operationalisation Matters

Machine learning cannot directly model an abstract concept such as  
"stability" without a measurable response definition.

Operationalisation establishes the bridge between:

Physical Concept  
↓  
Experimental Measurement  
↓  
Dataset Variable  
↓  
Predictive Target

## Interpretation

A foam half-life prediction should therefore be interpreted as a  
measurable indicator of foam stability within the defined research  
methodology.

It should not automatically be treated as representing every possible  
dimension of foam stability.

## Scope Boundary

The research states that stability is operationalised **primarily**  
through foam half-life.

This wording is important because it does not establish that foam  
half-life is the only possible stability measure.
