# THK-0008 - HPHT Conditions

## Definition

High-pressure/high-temperature reservoir conditions; the supplied literature cites classifications above approximately 150°C and 69 MPa.

## Core Idea

These conditions can substantially alter gas behaviour, drainage, viscosity, stability and formulation performance.

## Why It Matters

Defines the demanding operating domain of the research.

## Structure / Characteristics

High P + high T → altered phase/interfacial behaviour → altered foam performance.

## Governing Variables / Conditions

The object should be interpreted through the operating conditions, formulation variables, structural state, and engineering context represented in the connected notes.

## Engineering Application
