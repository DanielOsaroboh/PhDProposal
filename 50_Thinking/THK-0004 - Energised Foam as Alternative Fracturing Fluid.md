# THK-0004 - Energised Foam as Alternative Fracturing Fluid

## Strategy Definition

Use of gas-energised foam instead of conventional water-dominant fluid to reduce liquid loading while maintaining stimulation functions.

## Problem Addressed

The strategy targets water-sensitive and under-pressured reservoirs while preserving rheological support for proppant transport.

## Strategic Logic

```text
Select energised formulation → lower water fraction + useful foam rheology → potential operational advantage.
```

## Objective

Reduce disadvantages associated with water-dominant stimulation while retaining required fracturing-fluid functions.

## Potential Benefits

Connects the engineering problem to a deliberate fluid-design response.

## Conditions for Use

Suitability depends on reservoir sensitivity, pressure state, temperature, formulation performance, operational requirements, and validation.

## Trade-offs / Risks

Foam rheology and stability remain condition-sensitive; adopting energised foam does not remove the need for formulation optimisation and engineering assessment.
