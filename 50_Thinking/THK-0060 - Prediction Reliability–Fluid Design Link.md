# THK-0060 - Prediction Reliability–Fluid Design Link

## Relationship Definition

The reliability of predicted apparent viscosity and foam stability
affects the confidence with which engineers can make fracturing-fluid
formulation and operating-condition decisions.

## Upstream Information

Engineering design may depend on predictions of:

- [[THK-0005 - Foam Apparent Viscosity]];
- [[THK-0006 - Foam Stability]].

However, the predicted value alone does not fully describe its
engineering usefulness.

The reliability of that prediction also matters.

## Engineering Relationship

```text
Foam-property prediction
        +
Prediction reliability
        ↓
Confidence in predicted performance
        ↓
Confidence in fluid formulation
        +
Confidence in operating-condition selection
```
