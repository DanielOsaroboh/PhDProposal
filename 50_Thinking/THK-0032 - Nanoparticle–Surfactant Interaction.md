# THK-0032 - Nanoparticle–Surfactant Interaction

## Phenomenon

Physicochemical interaction between stabilising nanoparticles and surfactant species.

## Starting Condition

Performance depends on surface charge, surfactant chemistry and concentration, not nanoparticle presence alone.

## Causal Chain

```text
Surface chemistry + concentration → adsorption/aggregation/interfacial behaviour → stability response.
```

## Why the Mechanism Matters

Makes formulation interaction a primary modelling concern.

## Governing Variables

The strength and direction of the mechanism can depend on pressure, temperature, shear, foam quality, salinity, concentration, and formulation chemistry as relevant.

## Observable Outcome

The mechanism becomes visible through changes in foam structure, apparent viscosity, stability, drainage, or downstream engineering behaviour.

## Boundary Conditions

The causal pathway should not be assumed to have identical strength across formulations or outside tested operating ranges.

## Evidence

Gonzalez Perdomo and Wan Madihi (2022); Tran et al. (2023).
