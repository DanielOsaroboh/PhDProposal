# Knowledge Formation Protocol

## Purpose
Control the transformation of internally generated phenomena into transferable knowledge.

## Pipeline
OBS → PIA → DECOMP → INQ → INV → DISC → EXT

## Rules
1. Capture before explaining.
2. Interpret provisionally.
3. Decompose complexity before selecting a knowledge problem.
4. Bound the inquiry before gathering evidence.
5. Seek counter-evidence and competing explanations.
6. Distinguish findings from discoveries.
7. Do not claim global novelty without novelty assessment.
8. Extract the transferable structure from the originating case.
9. Classify candidate knowledge objects by function.
10. Create, enrich, or link Knowledge Assets with lineage preserved.
