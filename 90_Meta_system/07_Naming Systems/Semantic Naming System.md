# Semantic Naming System

## Processing Prefixes

- OBS — Observation
- PIA — Preliminary Interpretive Analysis
- DECOMP — Decomposition
- INQ — Inquiry
- INV — Investigation
- DISC — Discovery
- EXT — Knowledge Extraction

## Recommended Format

`PREFIX-XXX - Semantic Title`

Examples:

- `OBS-003 - The Tension Between Christ Reality and Pragmatism`
- `PIA-003 - Competing Authorities Governing Life`
- `DECOMP-003 - Christ Reality and Pragmatism Conflict`
- `INQ-003 - Can Christian Moral Orientation Coexist With Pragmatic Action`
- `INV-003 - Christian Moral Orientation and Pragmatic Action`
- `DISC-003 - My Conflict May Be a Functional Category Error`
- `EXT-003 - Functional Separation of Governing Authorities`

## Naming Rule

Name the object by its primary semantic content, not by the activity date or a vague topic label.
