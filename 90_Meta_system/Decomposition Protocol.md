# Decomposition Protocol

## Function
Expose the internal architecture of a complex phenomenon.

## Operations
D1. Elements — What things are present?
D2. Concepts — What ideas are hidden in the language?
D3. Tensions — What forces appear to compete?
D4. Mechanisms — What may be producing what?
D5. Assumptions — What is being treated as true?
D6. Claims — What propositions are being made?
D7. Questions — What must be understood?
D8. Knowledge Types — What candidate knowledge objects may emerge?

## Output
DECOMP note and inquiry candidates.
