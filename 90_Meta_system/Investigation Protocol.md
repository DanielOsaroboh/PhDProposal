# Investigation Protocol

## Function
Perform the epistemic work required to answer a defined inquiry.

## Operations
1. Define the inquiry precisely.
2. Record the initial position.
3. Identify claims and assumptions to test.
4. Identify candidate mechanisms.
5. Gather required evidence.
6. Deliberately seek counter-evidence.
7. Compare competing explanations.
8. Test proposed mechanisms.
9. Record contradictions and tensions.
10. Record belief revision.
11. Produce findings.
12. Identify candidate discoveries.

## Rule
Plausibility is not verification.

## Output
INV note and investigation findings.
