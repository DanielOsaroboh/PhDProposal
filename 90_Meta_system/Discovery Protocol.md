# Discovery Protocol

## Function
Articulate new or significantly clarified understanding emerging from investigation.

## Operations
1. State what became newly visible.
2. Classify the discovery type.
3. Record the previous mental model.
4. Record the emerging mental model.
5. Link supporting findings.
6. Explain the reasoning bridge.
7. State boundary conditions.
8. Record a counter-interpretation.
9. Calibrate epistemic and novelty status.
10. Assess transfer potential.

## Output
DISC note.
