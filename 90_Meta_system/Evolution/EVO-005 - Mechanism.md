A **mechanism** answers **"How does X produce Y?"**. It is fundamentally different from:

- **Concept**: _What is Foam Rheology?_
- **Principle**: _Stable foams improve proppant transport._
- **Process**: _How hydraulic fracturing is performed._
- **Mechanism**: _How pressure changes foam rheology._

For your ontology, this distinction will make the knowledge graph much more expressive and easier to navigate
