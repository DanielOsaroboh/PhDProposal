## The flow of knowledge

Your vault isn't just a collection of folders; it follows a pipeline:

```text
Capture
    ↓
Sources
    ↓
Processing
    ↓
Knowledge Objects
    ↓
Thinking
    ↓
Decision Systems
    ↓
Outputs
```

That means:

- **Capture** gathers raw information.
    
- **Sources** preserve original references.
    
- **Processing** transforms raw information into structured knowledge.
    
- **Knowledge Objects** store reusable, evergreen knowledge.
    
- **Thinking** reasons over that knowledge to answer questions and make decisions.
    
- **Application Assets** turn those decisions into tools and workflows.
    
- **Output Assets** produce papers, reports, presentations, and other deliverables.

## One architectural change I'd make

The one part I'd simplify is **10_Questions**. It overlaps with **09_Thinking** because both are organized around questions. Since Thinking notes already start with a question and then develop reasoning, I would eventually merge **10_Questions** into **09_Thinking**.
