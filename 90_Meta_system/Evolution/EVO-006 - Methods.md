## A refinement for your ontology

As your vault grows, I recommend splitting **Methods** into two subcategories because they serve different roles:

```
07_Methods
│
├── Modelling Methods
│   ├── Bayesian Optimisation
│   ├── Cross Validation
│   ├── Grid Search
│   ├── Monte Carlo Simulation
│
└── Analysis Methods
    ├── Uncertainty Quantification
    ├── Sensitivity Analysis
    ├── Feature Importance Analysis
```

- **Modelling Methods** are techniques used to build, train, or optimise models.
- **Analysis Methods** are techniques used to evaluate, interpret, or analyse models and data.

This distinction will make your knowledge base more intuitive and easier to navigate as you add more AI, statistics, and engineering methods.
