## A recommendation for Concept notes

For your ontology, every **Concept** note should answer the same seven questions:

1. **What is it?** (Definition)
2. **How does it work?** (Explanation)
3. **What are its key properties?** (Characteristics)
4. **Why is it important?** (Importance)
5. **How does it relate to other concepts?** (Relationships)
6. **Where is it used?** (Applications)
7. **Can I see a concrete example?** (Examples)

Using this consistent structure will make every concept in your vault immediately understandable and easy to compare across different domains.
