## One suggestion for your ontology

I recommend distinguishing **descriptive principles** from **prescriptive principles**:

```
06_Principles
│
├── Descriptive
│   ├── Stable Foams Improve Transport
│   ├── Pressure Influences Foam Rheology
│
└── Prescriptive
    ├── AI Supports Engineering Judgement
    ├── Engineering Decisions Should Consider Uncertainty
```

- **Descriptive principles** explain how the world works (scientific or engineering truths).
- **Prescriptive principles** recommend how people should think or act (guidelines for practice).

This distinction becomes very valuable as your vault grows because it separates natural laws and engineering relationships from best practices and decision-making guidance.
