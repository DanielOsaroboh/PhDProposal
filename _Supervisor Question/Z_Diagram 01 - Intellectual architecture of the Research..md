- These are my **primary predictive targets**.
- ML is used to capture the complex nonlinear relationships.
- [[THK-0028 - Predictive Uncertainty|Uncertainty]] is quantified because prediction alone does not establish reliability.
- Reliable predictions are combined with engineering constraints to identify [[THK-0103 - Uncertainty-Bounded Operating Window|candidate operating windows]].
- These windows support fracturing-fluid and proppant assessment rather than replace engineering judgement.
