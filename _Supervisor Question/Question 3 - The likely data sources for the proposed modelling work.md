
# Summary 
> **My primary source will be published experimental HPHT foam studies, supported by supplementary research data and publicly available datasets. Research-group data may also be incorporated if available and approved. Because these datasets are likely to be heterogeneous, an important part of the research will be extracting, digitising, harmonising and standardising them into a model-ready dataset.**



# Notes
### 1. Published Experimental Studies

- My **primary data source** will be published experimental studies on HPHT energised foam rheology and stability.
    
- I will extract reported measurements of **apparent viscosity, foam stability, pressure, temperature, foam quality, shear rate, salinity, surfactant concentration, and nanoparticle concentration**.
    

### 2. Supplementary Research Data

- I will use **supplementary datasets and data files** published alongside journal articles where available.
    
- These are preferable because they may provide more complete experimental observations than the main paper.
    

### 3. Publicly Available Research Datasets

- I will search for relevant **open-access or publicly available foam rheology datasets**.
    
- These will only be included where the experimental conditions and variables are relevant to the research.
    

### 4. Research-Group Data

- I may use **research-group or collaborator datasets** if they become available and are appropriately approved.
    
- However, the project will **not depend on proprietary or currently unavailable datasets**.
    

### 5. Data Preparation

- Because the available literature data are likely to be **small, heterogeneous and formulation-specific**, I will systematically **extract, digitise, harmonise and standardise** them.
    
- These sources will then be combined, where scientifically appropriate, into a **structured master HPHT foam dataset** for modelling.
