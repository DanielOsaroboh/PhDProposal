## **Purpose:** 
Explains **how I will actually carry out the research**.


- I begin with published experimental, supplementary and available research data.
- I extract, digitise, harmonise and standardise the data into a [[THK-0116 - Structured HPHT Foam Data Resource|structured HPHT dataset]].
- I assess whether the dataset is adequate before deciding the final modelling complexity.
- ML models predict [[THK-0005 - Foam Apparent Viscosity|apparent viscosity]] and [[THK-0006 - Foam Stability|foam stability]].
- I validate the models and distinguish [[THK-0096 - Interpolation–Extrapolation Distinction|interpolation from extrapolation]].
- I quantify uncertainty and assess [[THK-0074 - Uncertainty-Based Model Reliability Assessment|model reliability]].
- Reliable predictions are combined with engineering constraints to identify [[THK-0103 - Uncertainty-Bounded Operating Window|candidate operating windows]].
- The final outputs support engineering and further laboratory evaluation.
