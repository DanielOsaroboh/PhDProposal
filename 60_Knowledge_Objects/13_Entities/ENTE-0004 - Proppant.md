# Definition

A proppant is a solid material injected into fractures to keep them open after hydraulic fracturing.

# Characteristics

- High strength
- Spherical or angular
- Crush resistant

# Importance

Maintains fracture conductivity after pumping stops.

# Applications

- Hydraulic fracturing
