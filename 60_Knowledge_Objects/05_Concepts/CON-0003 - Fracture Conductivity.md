# Definition

Fracture conductivity is the ability of a hydraulic fracture to transmit fluids after stimulation.

# Explanation

It depends on fracture width, fracture length, proppant distribution, and the ability of the proppant pack to maintain open flow paths.

# Characteristics

- Hydraulic property
- Dynamic
- Pressure dependent
- Influenced by proppant placement

# Importance

High fracture conductivity increases hydrocarbon production by improving fluid flow from the reservoir.
