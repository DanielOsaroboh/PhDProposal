# Definition

Foam rheology is the study of how foam flows and deforms when subjected to external forces such as pressure and shear.

# Explanation

Unlike simple liquids, foam exhibits complex rheological behaviour because it consists of gas bubbles dispersed within a liquid phase. Its behaviour depends on pressure, temperature, foam quality, surfactants, and nanoparticles.

# Characteristics

- Non-Newtonian behaviour
- Apparent viscosity
- Yield stress
- Elasticity
- Time-dependent behaviour

# Importance

Understanding foam rheology is essential for designing stable hydraulic fracturing fluids capable of transporting proppants under reservoir conditions.
