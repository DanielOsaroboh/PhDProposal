# Definition

Pressure is the force applied per unit area.

# Unit

Pascal (Pa), MPa, psi

# Range

Depends on operating conditions.

# Influence

Pressure affects foam stability, viscosity, and gas compression.

# Measured By

Pressure gauges and downhole sensors.
