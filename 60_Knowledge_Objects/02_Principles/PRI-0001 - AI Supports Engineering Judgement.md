Practice 
# Statement

Artificial intelligence should support engineering judgement rather than replace it.

# Meaning

AI provides predictions, recommendations, and insights that assist engineers in making informed decisions. Final decisions should remain guided by engineering expertise, physical principles, and domain knowledge.

# Why It Matters

- Reduces blind reliance on algorithms.
- Ensures engineering accountability.
- Improves trust in AI-assisted systems.

# Applications

- Hydraulic fracturing
- Structural engineering
- Medical diagnosis
- Financial risk assessment

# Examples

Machine learning predicts foam viscosity, but engineers determine whether the prediction is physically reasonable before applying it.
