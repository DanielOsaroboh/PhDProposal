Descriptive
# Statement

Stable foams improve the suspension and transport of proppants during hydraulic fracturing.

# Meaning

The more stable a foam remains under operating conditions, the more effectively it can carry and distribute proppant through the fracture network.

# Why It Matters

- Increases fracture effectiveness.
- Reduces premature proppant settling.
- Improves fracture conductivity.

# Applications

- Hydraulic fracturing-fluid design.
- HPHT reservoir stimulation.
- Foam optimisation.

# Examples

A nanoparticle-stabilised foam maintains sufficient viscosity under HPHT conditions, allowing proppants to remain suspended.
