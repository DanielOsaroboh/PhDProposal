## One refinement to your ontology

As we decompose research papers, proposals, and textbooks, we'll encounter recurring items that don't fit neatly into concepts or entities alone. I recommend expanding your ontology to include these additional categories:

```
01_Concepts
02_Entities
03_Frameworks
04_Mechanisms
05_Processes
06_Principles
07_Methods
08_Models
09_Variables
10_Metrics
11_Problems
12_Solutions
13_Relationships
14_Evidence
15_Case Studies
```

This structure makes it much easier to organize and retrieve research knowledge. For example, "Uncertainty Quantification" can be stored as a **Method**, "Pressure" as a **Variable**, "RMSE" as a **Metric**, and "Limited Experimental Data" as a **Problem**, rather than forcing everything into generic concept notes.
