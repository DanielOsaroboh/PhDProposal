# Internal Knowledge Hub

> Internally generated knowledge processing from observation, experience, reflection, reasoning, and questions.

## Knowledge Formation Pipeline

1. **OBS — Observation**: What did I notice?
2. **PIA — Preliminary Interpretive Analysis**: What may be happening?
3. **DECOMP — Decomposition**: What is this made of?
4. **INQ — Inquiry**: What specifically must I understand?
5. **INV — Investigation**: What does the evidence support?
6. **DISC — Discovery**: What can I now see?
7. **EXT — Knowledge Extraction**: What is transferable?

## Governing Rule
Do not promote intelligent-sounding interpretation directly into a Knowledge Asset. Investigation is the verification barrier; Extraction is the transformation layer.


# External Knowledge Hub

> Knowledge entering the system from outside the system owner.

## Classes
1. **Sources** — Original external information objects.
2. **Source Notes** — What a specific source says.
3. **Literature Notes** — What external knowledge helps explain about a concept or problem.
4. **Source Syntheses** — What multiple sources collectively indicate.

## Flow
**Source → Source Note → Literature Note → Synthesis → Extraction → Knowledge Asset**
