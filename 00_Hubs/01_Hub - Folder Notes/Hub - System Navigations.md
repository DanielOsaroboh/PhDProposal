Your ontology is essentially organized around the classic **5W1H** framework plus evaluation:

|Question|Ontology|
|---|---|
|**What?**|Concepts, Entities|
|**Who/Which?**|Entities, Models|
|**How?**|Processes, Methods, Mechanisms|
|**Why?**|Principles, Mechanisms|
|**Where?**|Applications (within notes)|
|**When?**|Conditions, Processes (where applicable)|
|**How well?**|Metrics|
|**With what settings?**|Parameters|
|**For what purpose?**|Functions|

|Ontology|Primary Question|Example|
|---|---|---|
|**Concepts**|**What is it?**|What is Foam Rheology?|
|**Entities**|**What is the object?**|What is a Proppant?|
|**Processes**|**How is it done?**|How is Feature Engineering performed?|
|**Mechanisms**|**How does it work?**|How does pressure affect foam rheology?|
|**Principles**|**Why does it happen?**|Why do stable foams improve transport?|
|**Methods**|**How do we study or solve it?**|How is Cross Validation used?|
|**Models**|**Which model predicts or represents it?**|Which model is suitable for foam prediction?|
|**Variables**|**What changes?**|Which variables influence foam stability?|
|**Parameters**|**What settings control the model?**|What does the learning rate control?|
|**Metrics**|**How do we measure success?**|What does RMSE measure?|
|**Functions**|**What is its purpose?**|What is the function of Engineering Decision Support?|


- **Ontology** answers **"What do I know?"**
- **Decision Notes** answer **"Given what I know, what should I do?"**
- **Projects and Outputs** answer **"How do I apply or communicate that decision?"**


The flow becomes:

- **Knowledge Layer** → stores facts and concepts.
- **Decision Layer** → synthesizes knowledge to answer "What should I do?"
- **Output Layer** → communicates the decision in a thesis, report, presentation, or article.
- **Ontology** stores **what you know**.
- **Thinking** is where you **reason over what you know**.
- **Decision Systems** turn that reasoning into **actionable recommendations**.
- **Projects** apply those decisions to a specific goal.
- **Outputs** communicate the results
