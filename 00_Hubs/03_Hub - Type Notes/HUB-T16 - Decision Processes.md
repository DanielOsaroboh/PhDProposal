# HUB-T16 - Decision Processes

## Purpose

This HUB collects knowledge objects that answer:

> **How is evidence converted into an engineering decision?**

A decision process defines how predictions, evidence, criteria, constraints, and uncertainty are converted into a structured choice.

## Decision Process Pattern

```text
Evidence + Predictions
        ↓
Uncertainty Assessment
        ↓
Engineering Criteria
        ↓
Screening / Comparison
        ↓
Decision Output
```
