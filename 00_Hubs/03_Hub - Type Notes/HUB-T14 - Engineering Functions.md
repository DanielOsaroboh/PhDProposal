# HUB-T14 - Engineering Functions

## Purpose

This HUB collects knowledge objects that answer:

> **What engineering job must the system perform?**

An engineering function describes a required operational role that a material, component, or system must perform.

## Engineering Function Pattern

```text
Engineering Objective
        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
```
