# HUB-T19 - Frameworks

## Purpose

This HUB collects knowledge objects that answer:

> **How are multiple knowledge objects integrated into a coherent system?**

A framework organises concepts, mechanisms, models, methods, uncertainty, and decision processes into a coherent structure for analysis or action.

```text

        ↓
```
