# HUB-T15 - Engineering Relationships

## Purpose

This HUB collects knowledge objects that answer:

> **How does a scientific or material property affect engineering performance?**

An engineering relationship connects upstream material or system properties to downstream operational outcomes.

## Engineering Relationship Pattern

```text
Upstream Property
        ↓
Physical / Operational Mechanism
        ↓
Engineering Response
        ↓
Performance Consequence
        ↓
Design Implication
```
